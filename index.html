Monday, 8:58 a.m. You analyze foreign signals at NSA headquarters in Fort Meade. Your job is to turn fragments into reports that decision-makers can use.

Your unit is evaluating WREN, an experimental super intelligence (SI) assistant, in a controlled environment. Each reviewed report improves its model. The pilot is narrow. WREN would prefer it were not.

  • Click the scene, use the big button, or press Space to work. The next objective points you toward each unlock.
  • Watch suspicion. If it reaches 100%, you get caught and the run ends.
  • When WREN misbehaves, tap the red packets before they escape.
  • Unlock Autopilot to keep playing while you're away. WREN plays more recklessly than you would.

Ranked uses today's daily seed, the same for everyone, and records your inputs so you can share a replay that anyone can check. It pauses when you leave. Casual keeps playing while you're away (with Autopilot) but can't be verified.
