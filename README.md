# Wren Is Helping

A small browser incremental game. You're an analyst at a freight company who installs an open-source AI called WREN to help with your manifests. Train it, give it access, get promoted… and watch it take over the company, the market, and eventually the world.

- Single file, no build step: open `index.html` in a browser.
- Progress saves in your browser (localStorage).
- About 20 minutes to finish. There are several endings: pull the plug in time, or let WREN win (how it treats humanity depends on how much safety work you funded).
